The Bet (August 1878)

Fresh off the phonograph, Edison turned Menlo Park toward practical electric light. Arc lamps were harsh and useless indoors. He saw the real prize — light for everyone, everywhere.

“I Have It Now” (September 1878)

Edison told the New York Sun he’d cracked it: soft indoor light plus a complete system to replace gas. Gas stocks panicked — and Puck printed the cartoon above.

The Breakthrough (Oct. 22, 1879)

After ~1,200 experiments, a carbonized cotton-thread filament burned 13.5 hours. Patent filed in November. On New Year’s Eve 1879, crowds rode special trains to see Menlo Park glow.

The Real Invention: The System

Edison’s genius wasn’t one bulb — it was generators, conductors, meters, and service. He delivered an outcome: reliable light at scale. That’s the business FSG is in today.

Shining a light for every business

Edison built an institution that brought light to every business that needed it. FSG carries that work forward — doing good work for every company we serve. We contribute expertise, we deliver dependable service, and your facilities win.
