Why This Play Matters

Every rep asks the same question: "do we have a story like this customer?" The answer is always yes. It's just buried in Engage notes, invoices, and somebody's memory. Proof Builder is how your win stops being tribal knowledge and becomes ammunition the whole org can fire.

And it keeps score. Every card carries ★ Proof submitted by [your name], on the card, in the pop-out, in the deck the next rep builds off it. Fifteen minutes of your time buys a permanent credit line.

One card, three places. Your proof lands in the Results grid, makes the client's chip clickable on Products × Delivery, and enters the library the deck generator pulls from. You fill one form.

The Five Steps

Development chip → Proof Builder. Fill it in order. It takes longer to find the files than to write the words.

1

Tell The Story

Submitted by / your name / your email. This is the credit line. Don't skip it.

Client name — legal-ish and consistent. "Ulta Beauty", not "Ulta".

Vertical — one of thirteen, Commercial Real Estate & Office through Multifamily. Pick how the customer would describe themselves, not how we file them.

The cell — Product line (Electrical · Lighting · Signs · Technology Infrastructure · Energy Performance) × Delivery method (Construction · Service · Distribution) × Type. This is how the engine matches your proof to the next prospect, so get it right: a lighting retrofit tagged "Electrical" surfaces for the wrong buyer.

The problem — their words, their pain, before we showed up. Two or three sentences. Don't name FSG here.

The solution — what we did, scope and scale. Outcome first, then how.

Client quote & attribution — one real sentence from a real person with their real title. Spell the name right; we verify it.

Multi-scope job? One card per cell. A rebrand rollout with sign fabrication and electrical service is two proofs with their own dollars — and two roster spots instead of one.
2

Load The Numbers

Numbers are what separate a proof card from a testimonial.

★ Gold stat — dollars first. The headline number, and it should be money if money is available: contract value, annual savings, cost avoided.

Every stat needs a number and a label. "328 locations", "18 weeks", "42% energy reduction". A stat slot is not a place for a program name — that goes in the Program field.

Only claims the record supports. Pull from the order, the invoice, or approved site copy. Nothing estimated, nothing rounded up. If it isn't in the record, leave it out.

3

Drop The Files

Client logos — up to 4. Transparent PNG or SVG if you have it.

Job photos — up to 6. Shoot landscape and get the work in frame: fixture, sign, panel, gear. Not an empty parking lot. Keep them tight; the gold frame comes from the card itself.

Proposal / backup link — the Engage opportunity or SmartSheet URL that proves the numbers.

Google Drive link — the folder or file behind the job: the proposal, the full photo set, the source. Check it's shared before you paste it — a link QA can't open is the same as no link.

Nobody sees either but QA; they're what make the card auditable.

4

Check Permissions

Four toggles, and they're the ones that keep us out of trouble: name approved · logo approved · numbers approved · quote approved.

You attest to them as the submitter — so know the answer, don't guess it.

Anything not approved renders anonymized — "a national beauty retailer, 1,400 stores". Still useful, still proof.

Scrub as you write. No portal URLs, logins, passwords, or personal contact info in any free-text field. Engage records are full of them; proof cards never are.

5

Send It

Four buttons at the bottom. The last one submits — the other three are for you.

Email Growth Hub — opens an email to Bobby with you CC'd, so you always have your own copy of what you sent. Attach the proof card that downloads alongside it, then send.

Download Proof Card — your finished HTML card. Yours to show a client today, before it's even published.

Copy Deck Snippet — drop the proof straight into a deck or one-pager.

Copy Proof Record — the paste-ready record for the engine. Grab it if QA asks.

While the direct send is being switched on: the Email button downloads the card and opens your email instead. Attach the downloaded card before you hit send — check your Downloads folder. The full write-up is on your clipboard; paste it into the body. Once the direct send is live this step disappears and the card attaches itself.

The QA Pass

Scrub, copy into FSG voice, stat repair, photo crop, quote and cell verification. Then it's approved and flips to LIVE. Turnaround is days, not weeks. If something's missing, you'll hear which field — one email, one fix, done.

★

You Get Told

When your proof goes live, you get an email saying so. You don't have to go looking for it, and you don't have to wonder whether it landed.

What Makes A Card Land

Do

  • Lead the gold stat with money — contract value, annual savings, cost avoided.
  • Write the problem in their words, before FSG shows up.
  • Tag the cell the way the next buyer would search for it.
  • Shoot photos landscape with the work in frame.
  • Split a multi-scope job into one card per cell.

Don't

  • Round a number up, or estimate one the record doesn't support.
  • Put a program name in a stat slot.
  • Paste a portal URL, login, or personal contact into a free-text field.
  • Guess on a permission toggle. Know it.
  • Leave the submitted-by fields blank — that's your name off the board.

Common Misses

  • Blank submitted-by — the card goes up uncredited. The whole recognition loop runs off that one field.
  • Wrong cell — the proof surfaces for the wrong buyer, or never surfaces at all. It's the single field most worth double-checking.
  • A stat with no label — "328" on its own means nothing on a card a stranger is reading in a meeting.
  • Permissions guessed, not known — the fastest way to turn a good proof into a legal conversation.
  • One card for a three-scope job — the dollars get muddled and you take one roster spot instead of three.

The Goal

Sixteen open roster spots. Every one of them is a win somebody on this team already delivered and nobody else can use yet. The gap between a job you closed and a proof the org can fire is about fifteen minutes of your time.

Fifteen minutes. One form. Your name on it permanently. Contribution + Service = Value.
