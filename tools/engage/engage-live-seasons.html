ENGAGE LIVE — Seasons 1 & 2

Two Seasons · 24 Episodes · Live on Zoom · 30 Minutes · Clients as the Hero

The Exceptional Experience with Technology. This is not software training — it's how we run the business. Every episode connects a piece of Engage to the job it powers, the teammate it serves, and the client it ultimately helps win.

The One Sheet engage.fsgapps.com

Why Engage Live Exists

Engage Live is FSG's enterprise webinar series on how we use Engage — our CRM and technology launch pad — to deliver an exceptional experience for our clients and excel in every role across the company.

Our clients feel our technology even when they never see it. When every conversation is captured, every commitment tracked, and every team working from the same record, the client experiences one seamless FSG — prepared, responsive, and impossible to surprise. That's the exceptional experience, and Engage is what makes it possible at scale.

The Story We Tell

At FSG, the client is the hero, and we are the guide. Every pursuit, project, and renewal follows the same arc:

ProblemWe start where the client starts — the facility challenge, the risk, the cost, the frustration. The client's problem is the opening scene of every story we tell.
SolutionFSG shows up as the expert guide. Engage is how we show up prepared — every conversation, quote, and commitment captured so the client never repeats themselves and never wonders where things stand.
ResultsThe client wins: savings delivered, facilities running, promises kept. Their success is the ending — and in Engage, it becomes the proof that opens the next story.
Engage is the launch pad. Every episode answers the same question: how does this feature, this role, or this handoff help us tell that story better? Engage isn't the point — the client's result is the point.

A.B.C. — Always Be Capturing

Every great sales story has its mantra. Ours is a friendly remake of the classic: Always Be Capturing. Every conversation logged, every commitment tracked, every opportunity in Engage — because a deal that isn't captured can't be closed, coached, forecasted, or delivered.

Every episode carries a line from The Closer's Creed — 24 original mantras, one per episode, written with a wink to the greatest sales movie ever made. First prize: your pipeline runs itself. Second prize: a set of steak knives. Third prize: you're still living in spreadsheets.

How the Series Works

  • Live webinars on Zoom, 30 minutes, open to all of FSG Enterprise — every role, every division.
  • Live in Engage the whole time. Real records, real workflows, real speed — no slide decks.
  • Every episode opens with a Play of the Week: one teammate, one real client win powered by Engage.
  • Every episode closes with Your One Move: a single action every attendee takes before the next session.
  • Season One masters the tool. Season Two masters the machine — every role, division, and handoff in the enterprise.
