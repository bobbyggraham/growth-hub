The 30-Second Race

The session opened with a race: find a random account, a random contact, and an open opportunity — live, on the clock — in under 30 seconds. The challenge stands for every rep who watches the tape: by the end of this page, you'll beat that time. Not because you're faster at clicking — because you'll know where everything lives.

The Dashboard Tour — Every Panel Feeds Your Number

The dashboard isn't a splash screen — it's mission control. Every panel answers one question a closer asks daily:

1

My Accounts — Your Book at a Glance

What you see is what you own. Your book of business in one view — and if an account you work isn't in it, that's a Request Assignment play, not a shrug.

2

Tasks & Follow-Ups — Today's At-Bats

The heartbeat panel. Every open lead and opportunity carries a follow-up date, and this is where they line up each morning. An empty task panel doesn't mean you're done — it means nothing's dated.

3

Pipeline & Opportunities — The Scoreboard

Open opportunities by stage, value, and close date — the number your forecast, your manager, and your commission all read from. Keep the fields real and this panel tells the truth.

★

Customize It — Pin What You Touch Daily

Your home view is yours. Pin My Accounts and your open Tasks panel; hide what you don't touch. Mission control only works when the instruments you need are in front of you.

Find Anything — Global Search in Under 10 Seconds

The search bar at the top of every screen reads across people, leads, accounts, and opportunities at once. The skill is knowing which swing to take:

You're huntingThe moveWhy it works
A record you can nameType the exact name in global searchExact match surfaces straight to the top — one click, you're in. Check Top Matches, not just the tab you're on.
A name you half-rememberPartial search, then the module's Contains filterPartial match casts the wide net. In Accounts, filter the name column both ways — the paper prefix and the end-user name — so a misnamed record can't hide.
A person, not a companySearch the human, read every match typePeople surface as leads and contacts. Finding which one they already are is the difference between a clean convert and a duplicate.
What's gone quietSort a module by Last Activity, oldest firstDormant accounts and aging leads are money sitting in plain sight — search is also how you re-open conversations.
The rule.  Search before you create — always. Every duplicate account, twin contact, and split pipeline in the system started with somebody skipping a ten-second search.

Beat the Clock — The Mission Control Checklist

Run it right now, in Engage, on the clock:

0 of 6 — still on the launchpad

Do / Don't

Do

  • Search Top Matches first — every record type, one query.
  • Pin My Accounts and Tasks to your home view and start every day there.
  • Run the Contains filter both ways when a name might be entered differently.
  • Use search to hunt dormant records — quiet accounts are found money.
  • Time yourself. Under ten seconds to any record is the standard.

Don't

  • Create anything before you've searched for it. Ten seconds beats a duplicate forever.
  • Scroll a 78,000-row module looking for a record you could search in one line.
  • Live in one tab — a person can be a lead, a contact, or both.
  • Leave the default dashboard untouched. Stock instruments fly nobody's mission.
  • Bookmark your way around the app. The rail and the search bar are faster.
