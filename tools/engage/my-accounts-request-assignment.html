Engage Plays

My Accounts + Request Assignment Button

What You See and Why  •  Sales Flow  •  Direct Sales Team

One rule drives the whole view: if your name is on it, it's in "My Accounts." Engage pulls in everything assigned to you at two levels — Master Accounts and Sites. You will not see accounts for opportunities you are not assigned to at the account or site level.

The Two Levels

1. Master Accounts

You are the assigned Account Manager on the master account record. The master — and everything under it — lives in your view. This is the full relationship.

2. Sites

You are assigned at the site level. That site shows in your view even when another AM owns the master account. You own the site relationship; they own the master.

What You See = What You Own

Master AccountThe master, all sites, and all opportunities under itThe full customer relationship

Requesting a Change

Are you working on an account or site you don't see? You're not assigned to it in Engage.

Click the "Request Assignment" button in Engage — request assignment to the account or site right from the record, and it gets approved or denied by Sales Leadership.

  • Request Assignment — Button
  • The change request is sent to Sales Leadership — Rep # and name: "Change Sales Rep to #1245 — Jane Smith."
  • Why — If the account or site is not assigned to you, you will not be assigned the transaction that was opened.

The Request Flow, Step by Step

  1. Start from the record you actually want

    Where you click is what you get. Want one site? Open the Sites tab, click into that site, and hit Request Assignment from the site record — the request points at that site and nothing else. Start from the master account and you're asking for the whole thing: every site, every opportunity under it.

    • One location, one customer of yours — go to the site.
    • The full relationship — start at the master account.
    • Don't ask for the account to get one site. That's how you end up owning 40 locations you've never called on.

  2. Confirm the scope: single site or whole account

    Engage asks you to say it out loud. Pick Single site or Whole account, and the request is scoped to exactly that. Then it runs a check against the systems before anything goes to leadership.

  3. Continue — and Engage runs the analysis

    On Continue, Engage looks across the ERP and CRM for evidence you're already working this customer. Four signals, no opinions:

    SignalWhat Engage looks atSource
    Recent work ordersPosted or open in the past 6 monthsERP
    Construction jobsAssigned to you for this customerERP
    Open opportunitiesOwned by youEngage
    Recent winsClosed won in the past 90 daysEngage

    The check is scoped to whatever you picked in Step 2 — request a single site and it evaluates your history at that site.

  4. Read the verdict — then send

    Two outcomes. Neither one stops you: the button says Continue either way. What changes is how much work your email has to do.

    Green — history found

    "The spreadsheet has spoken."

    • Engage found your name on the work.
    • Continue Anyway sends a request that defends itself.
    • Expect a fast approval.
    Amber — nothing found

    "The CRM couldn't find your name on the victory lap."

    • All four signals came back zero.
    • You can still Continue — but you're making the case in writing.
    • Better move: log the activity, then request.

  5. The email goes to Sales Leadership

    The request lands with the scope you chose, the four signals as Engage found them, and your case. Leadership approves or denies from there.

Sales needs to own something to run a victory lap. The evaluation isn't a gate — it's a mirror. If Engage can't find a work order, a job, an opportunity, or a win with your name on it, then you're asking leadership to take your word for it on an account you have no visible history with. Put the work in the system first. Then the request makes itself.

Who Updates What

Sales Leadership is responsible for updating the Account Class and the Rep ID. Fix it at the source once — the Account and Sites — and new WOs come out right automatically.
