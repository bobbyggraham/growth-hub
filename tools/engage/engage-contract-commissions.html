Engage Contract Commissions: How It Works

You sign it, you enter it. The click-by-click path from a signed contract on a Closed Won opportunity to an approved commission record — for submitters, VPs, managers, and reps.
FACILITY SOLUTIONS GROUP
Growth Organization  ·  2026

01  The Flow at a Glance

The one-line version: a rep adds the signed contract to the opportunity in Engage. A VP approves it. Approval creates a commission record for each rep on the contract. The rep and the rep’s manager each approve that record before it goes to payroll.
StepWhoWhat happens in Engage
1Contract submitter (sales rep)Adds the contract on the opportunity’s Orders tab and uploads the signed contract. Status: Draft.
2Contract approver (VP)Reviews the contract and clicks Approve under Manager Approval.
3Engage and the ERPCreate a contract commission record for each rep on the contract.
4Sales repReviews the record and sets their approval to Approved or Change Requested.
5Sales rep managerReviews the record and sets the manager approval to Approved or Change Requested. Steps 4 and 5 can happen in either order.
Who gets paid on contracts: only reps whose commission trigger is Contract Execution. These reps are paid once, on the contract amount. Jobs, work orders, and orders billed later still appear on their statements, but with $0 commission — nobody is paid twice. A rep with a blank commission setup gets the default plan (Salary Draw and Revenue Recognition) and will see $0 from contracts.

Applies to: contracts executed October 2026 and later  ·  System: Engage (engage.fsgapps.com)

02  Contract Submitter Sales Rep

You enter every signed contract yourself, starting with October 2026. There are no more mass uploads. Three steps: open, fill, upload.

1

Open the Opportunity’s Orders Tab

Closed Won Opportunity→ Orders→ ✎  Add Contract

Open the Closed Won opportunity the contract belongs to, click the Orders tab, then click Add Contract above the Orders grid.

2

Fill In the Contract

FieldWhat to enter
Contract NameA name people will recognize, such as the site address. This name shows in the Invoice # column on commission statements.
Contract AmountThe pre-tax contract amount.
Contract Execution DateThe date the contract was signed. This sets the commission month.
Sales Rep 1 + Commission (%)Search by name or rep number. Enter the percent as a number: 0.3 means 0.30%.
Sales Rep 2 + Commission (%)Only if a second rep shares the deal. The percent box unlocks after you pick a rep.

After you save, the contract shows in the Orders grid with Type Contract and Status Draft.

3

Upload the Signed Contract

Orders grid→ Contract name→ Detail panel→ Upload signed contract

Click the contract name to open its detail panel, then drag the signed contract into the upload box, or click browse. Engage detects the upload on its own. The approver can’t approve until the signed contract is attached.

Once the upload finishes, the file shows under the upload box with download and delete buttons, and the upload warning goes away. The contract stays in Draft. As the rep, you still can’t click Approve — the panel says Only a VP or above can approve contracts. Your part is done. The contract now waits for a VP.

Need to fix something? Click Edit on the contract panel before it is approved. After approval, contract commission records can’t be rebuilt from Engage — changes become a request to the ERP team.

03  Contract Approver VP

Contract approval is the main control on contract pay. Nothing is paid until you approve.

Opportunity→ Orders→ Contract detail panel→ ✓  Approve

Open the contract from the opportunity’s Orders tab, or from the notice you receive. Check the amount, execution date, reps, and commission percents against the signed contract in the panel. Then click Approve under Manager Approval. If something is wrong, don’t approve — send it back to the submitter to fix with Edit.

When you approve, Engage creates a contract commission record for each rep on the contract.

Who can approve: only a VP or above. Engage checks this by job code — anyone else sees the Approve button grayed out. Approver job codes: DV, XV, EVC, CE, CO, COC, COL, CFO, CI, CMO, CP, and CST.

04  Approving the Commission Record Sales Rep Manager

Each commission record has its own manager approval. The manager on a record comes from payroll (the rep’s reporting manager), not from Engage. If the wrong manager shows up, the payroll record needs fixing.

Commission→ Commission Statement / Global→ Contract row→ Approved / Change Requested

Go to Commission and open the rep’s Commission Statement, or the Global report. Find the contract rows: Application shows Contract and Invoice # shows the contract name. Set the manager approval to Approved if the record is right, or Change Requested if it isn’t.

05  Approving Your Own Record Sales Rep

Your approval is separate from your manager’s. Both show on each record.

Commission→ Your Commission Statement→ Contract row→ Approved / Change Requested

Go to Commission and open your Commission Statement. Find the contract row and check the contract name, month, and commission amount. Set your approval to Approved if it is right, or Change Requested if it isn’t.

06  Approval Statuses

StatusMeaning
PendingNot reviewed yet.
ApprovedThe reviewer agrees the record is correct.
Change RequestedThe reviewer found a problem that needs fixing before payroll.
