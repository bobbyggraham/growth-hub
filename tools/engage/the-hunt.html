Part 1 · Why

Let's Say It — Leads Are SH!T

There. Somebody had to. Time is the one thing you can't add to the pipeline, and the wrong leads quietly drain it — endless follow-up loops with contacts who were never going to move. Meanwhile a handful of leads in that same queue can transform the business: clear demand, clear timing, ready to partner, ready to scale across a national footprint.

The point isn't to hate leads — it's to stop treating them all the same. A closer's best energy goes where it compounds. The skill this episode builds is telling the difference fast — in the first conversation, not the fifth.

And here's the good news: the module itself is a weapon now. You watched us run it live in Episodes 3 and 4 — the machine does the typing so you can do the hunting:

Part 2 · What

First, Redefine the Word — A Lead Is a Relationship

Before the stars, kill the biggest myth in the queue: a lead is not a stranger's row in a database. A lead is a contact — a relationship being nurtured. It doesn't matter where the relationship stands or where the business comes from; if there's a person and a next possibility, it's a lead, and it gets worked like one:

New Engagement · New Business
The classic hunt — a brand-new contact, a brand-new opportunity. The badge scan, the web form, the cold call that landed.
Existing Account · New Business
A current customer, a new line — the lighting client who's never heard our EV or signage story. New product, same trust.
Existing Relationship · New Engagement
A referral, a new site, a new contact inside an account we already serve. The relationship opens the door; the lead still gets built.
Existing Account · Repeat Business
The reorder, the next phase, the renewal. Still a lead — still captured, still nurtured, still scored. Repeat business dies from being taken for granted.
All four are leads, and the test doesn't care which quadrant you're in. The five stars measure the relationship and the moment — not where it started. Nurture is the constant; the stars tell you how far the nurturing has come.

The 5-Star Lead

Five signals. Every lead in your queue is somewhere on this ladder — and every rung it climbs, the odds move your way:

★★★★★

Willing to Engage

They lean in. Calls get returned, the conversation moves both ways. Without this, you don't have a lead — you have a name.

★★★★★

Friendly and Collaborative

A working partner, not a gatekeeper. They share information, open doors, and want you to succeed on their problem.

★★★★★

Knows What They Want — and It's an FSG Product

Clear scope in a lane we run: lighting, electrical, signage, EV, technology. You're solving, not guessing.

★★★★★

Knows When They Want It

Real timeline, real urgency, real budget. A date on the calendar beats “sometime next year” every single time.

★★★★★

Knows They Want It From You

FSG is the choice — not one of five bids. They've seen the proof, they trust the team, and they're asking for the paper.

Five for five, you're not selling anymore — you're partnering. That's the lead you convert today and route toward a national program: Global Services, the GPO networks, the CRE partners. One 5-star lead inside those doesn't close a deal — it opens a footprint.

And Follow-Up date? Not a star — the heartbeat. Follow-Up is your organization strategy: the cadence and the dashboard tracker that keep you focused and on time. Selling on follow-up isn't a skill — it's intuition and consistency. The stars measure the lead; the Follow-Up date measures you. Every open lead carries one, always.

One thing the stars are not: Status. The Status lane — New Lead → First 48 → Contacting / Follow-Up → Qualified or Not Qualified — is the process clock, and Engage's AI helps keep it moving. It tells you where a lead is in the workflow, not how good it is: a 1-star lead can sit in Contacting forever, and a 5-star lead can land Qualified an hour after it's born. Run the process on Status; run the strategy on the stars — willing to engage, friendly & collaborative, knows what, knows when, from YOU. When the test hits four or five, Status catches up fast: Qualified, then Convert.

Coach's Corner — Organize Your Board for Five Stars

Now set the room up so the test runs itself. Fifteen minutes, once — and your Leads grid stops being a list and starts being a scoreboard:

1

Make the Grid Your Scorecard

Open My Leads and drag the columns until one row tells the whole story: Rating · Status · Source · Primary Interest · Est. Value · Follow-Up · Last Activity. Everything the 5-Star test reads, visible without opening a single record.

2

Make the Rating Your Star Column

That little icon column is the whole model at a glance: Hot (the gold cup) = 4–5 stars. Warm = 2–3. Cold (the snowflake) = 0–1. Re-rate on every touch — honestly, including down — and you can read your entire queue's star spread in one scroll.

3

Put the Score in the First Note

Open every lead's Notes with the score line — “★★★ — missing: timeline, from-us” — and update it on every touch. Any teammate reads the lead in one line, and the AI Brief writes next steps aimed at exactly the stars you're missing.

4

Save Three Views — Your Star Tiers

The Views menu at the top of the grid is your ladder. Save three: “The Five Star Board” — Hot + Qualified, follow-up this week (the convert list); “The Workbench” — Warm, every lead carrying a next action aimed at one missing star; “Going Gray” — Last Activity 14+ days or no follow-up date at all.

5

Run the Board in Order

Five Star Board first, every morning — convert what's ready before the coffee's cold. Workbench second — one star-earning move per lead. And Going Gray goes to zero every Friday: converted, disqualified, or dated. That's how your name stays off the aging report.

No new fields. No waiting on a release. Rating, Notes, Primary Interest, Est., Follow-Up, Source, Views — the 5-Star test runs on the record Engage already gives you. The features are live; the organization is the coaching; the discipline is yours.

Work the Queue — Daily Hygiene in Under 10 Minutes

The hunt is a habit, not an event. Run this every day and the aging report never prints your name:

0 of 8 — the queue is winning

Do / Don't

Do

  • Touch every new lead within 24 hours — the first voice usually wins.
  • Score before you chase. The five stars decide who gets your afternoon.
  • Convert 5-star leads the same day — and read the duplicate screen on the way through.
  • Aim every follow-up at one specific missing star, and log it as the next action.
  • Disqualify with a reason — a clean kill teaches the org where not to hunt.

Don't

  • Treat all 6,096 leads the same. Equal effort on unequal leads is how pipelines starve.
  • Let a lead age a week untouched — the aging report prints your name under it.
  • Grind on 1-star leads while a 4-star lead waits for a callback.
  • Delete instead of disqualify — deleted leads teach nobody anything.
  • Convert a bare name. Feed the record first, so the AI Brief and Enrich have something to work with.
