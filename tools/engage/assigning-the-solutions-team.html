Assigning the Solutions Team

Get the Right Expert on the Deal  •  Sales Team Quick Guide  •  Opportunities Module

Why This Play Matters

The Solutions Team turns your opportunity into a proposal — but only as fast and as accurately as the information you hand them. Right assignee, complete fields, docs uploaded: that's the difference between a quote in days and a stalled deal waiting on questions.

The Play at a Glance

1. Pick the Right Assignee
2. Complete the Required Fields
3. Upload the Docs
4. Assign & Notify
5. Watch for the 48-Hour Confirm
1
Pick the Right Assignee

Assignment follows the Product and Delivery Method on the opportunity — both required fields in Engage. Type is optional and multi-select; Product Owners add types. One owner per lane — they pull in additional team members if needed.

3
Upload the Docs

All files provided at the onset of the project go in the Files folder on the opportunity. The more information shared upfront, the faster and more accurate the proposal.

Turnkey Lighting

  • Specify the ask — ROI/proforma needed or just a quote? Turnkey or material-only?
  • Utility bill — upload a recent bill if ROI is needed.
  • Existing fixture info — lamp type, wattage, quantity — if the audit wasn't performed by the Solutions Team.

Rebates (for accurate estimates)

  • The package — full utility bill (name must match), signed LOA, signed proposal, line-by-line SOW.
  • A responsive POC — name, phone, email. Site maps if more than 200 lines.
  • Set expectations — processing takes 6–8 weeks. Do not guarantee estimates to customers.

Electrical

  • Plans & specs — upload if available; otherwise complete the Electrical Job Bidding Checklist.
  • Job type & scope — include voltage, amperage, footage, site conditions, and any other relevant details.
4
Assign & Notify
  • Assign per Step 1 — add team members only if the table specifies.
  • Send a follow-up — email or Slack the assignee with the opportunity link (recommended).
  • Rush request? Email David Burrough with CC to Frank Agraz & Chad Jones (Lighting), Cameron Carpenter (ECMs), or Steven Miller (Electrical).
5
Watch for the 48-Hour Confirm

The Solutions Team responds within 48 hours with confirmation, an ETA, and any questions. Monitor the Notes on the opportunity — unanswered questions stall the clock.

Project TypeTurnaround
Electrical Estimation7–10 business days
Budgetary / ROM Quotes3–5 business days
Turnkey LightingVaries — ETA provided within 48 hours

Common Misses — Check Before You Walk Away

  • Details scattered across notes — everything belongs in the Customer Problem field, one place, complete.
  • Bid Due Date set to a placeholder — the Solutions Team plans around it. Enter the real deadline.
  • David Burrough added by default — David is the escalation path. Every lane in the matrix has a Product Owner — route to them.
  • Rebate estimates promised to the customer — processing takes 6–8 weeks and estimates are never guaranteed.
  • ROI requested, no utility bill uploaded — no bill, no proforma. Upload it with the request.
  • Vague electrical scope — missing voltage, amperage, or footage means a round of questions before work starts.
