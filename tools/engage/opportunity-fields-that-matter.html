Why This Matters

Closers are on their game. To be effective, you need to know what to act on today — and if your pipeline is unclear, how do you know where to start? A clean pipeline is your game plan: open the dashboard, and your next move is already in front of you. No digging, no guessing, no wasted morning — it isn't admin — it's how closers stay ready.

Five minutes a day keeps your entire pipeline current. Know your next move before the coffee's cold.

One owner, one assignee, one follow-up date. The Owner owns the record from cradle to cash. The Assignee has the ball — it changes by stage. The Follow-Up Date is when the Owner needs it back.

Closing It Lost? Pick the Right Reason

Closed Lost requires a Lost Reason — it's how we learn where deals die. Pick the one that actually happened:

Lost ReasonWhat it means
No DecisionCustomer chose to do nothing — status quo won. No vendor was selected.
PricingWe were the right fit, but cost or perceived value didn't align with their budget or expectations.
CompetitorLost head-to-head to another vendor on fit, price, relationships, or timing.
Customer Not AwardedOur customer didn't win their project — the deal died upstream, not with us.
QualificationOpportunity wasn't real — bad fit, no budget authority, unclear need, or not ready to buy.
DuplicateRecord already exists. Housekeeping only.
Internal MisalignmentWe tripped over ourselves — Sales, Solutions, Ops, or Pricing weren't aligned on scope, fit, or delivery.

Three Easy Steps to Stay Healthy

Work your list top to bottom. No sorting, no overthinking — the Dashboard already puts the oldest in front of you.

1

Open Your Dashboard

Click the first opportunity on the list. That's it — start at the top.

2

Update the Follow-Up Date

When do you need to act next? Set the date. If there's no next action, ask yourself whether the deal is still alive.

3

Update the Estimated Close Date

When do they plan to buy? If the budget is unsure, push the close date to January of next year — and use your follow-up date to check in this year. While you're in there: if Estimated Value is blank or $0, enter $1 or a humble estimate until Operations gives you a real number.

Then click the next one. Repeat until the list is clear.

Common Misses

  • Blank follow-up date — the opportunity falls off your radar and off the dashboard. Nobody chases what nobody sees.
  • Close date in the past — the deal reads as dead or forgotten in every pipeline report, even if you're actively working it.
  • $0 or blank value — your deal contributes nothing to the dollar roll-up. Your pipeline looks empty when it isn't.
  • The $1 that never grows — the placeholder is a bridge, not a home. Update it the moment Operations gives you a real estimate.

The Practice — Base Hits Win Championships

You don't need a grand slam. Championships are won on base hits — small, consistent swings, every single day. Here's the practice:

D

Daily — Take Your Swings (5 min)

Open the Dashboard and run the three steps on the opportunities at the top of your list. A few base hits a day keeps your whole pipeline moving.

W

Weekly — Clear the Bases (Friday, 15 min)

Knock out past-due close dates and blank follow-up dates before the weekend. Don't leave runners stranded.

M

Monthly — Check Your Average (First Monday)

Scan your full open list. Every opportunity should have a follow-up date, a real close date, and a value. That's a 1.000 batting average — and it's the only stat leadership reads.

The Goal

Every opportunity current. Follow-up date set, close date real, value entered. If you're carrying a heavy pipeline, don't try to fix it all in one sitting — work within a 30–60-day window until you're caught up. Current beats perfect.

Base hits win championships. Healthy pipeline, honest forecast, visible work — that's what five minutes a day buys you.
