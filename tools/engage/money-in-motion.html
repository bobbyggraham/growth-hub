The Three States of Money

Every dollar FSG will ever bill passes through three states, and Engage tracks all three. Learn to read them and you can answer the only question that matters — where's my money? — in ten seconds flat:

1

Pipeline — Money You're Chasing

Every open opportunity, by stage, value, and close date. This is the future — what you've found, what you're working, what leadership forecasts from. Pipeline is only as real as the fields on it: a blank value or a past-due close date is noise, not money.

2

WIP — Money You're Earning

Work in progress: deals you closed-won that are now being executed. The sale is made, the money isn't home yet. WIP is where you watch a win turn into revenue — and where you spot the job that's stalled between sold and billed.

$

Earned — Money on the Board

Billed and booked. This is the number your YTD sales, your commission statement, and the scoreboard all read from. Earned is history you can't edit — which is exactly why the two states before it have to be right.

Read it like a closer: Pipeline tells you what's coming, WIP tells you what's landing, Earned tells you what counted. A healthy book has money in all three — all the time.

Once the board is clean, the namesake view shows you your money live. 💰 Money in Motion is a shared view in both Opportunities and Accounts — same picker, and it's ready for the whole team:

1

Open Opportunities (or Accounts), click the view picker, choose 💰 Money in Motion.

2

Filter FSG Owner to You

The shared view opens org-wide. Filter FSG Owner to yourself and it becomes your money — your open opportunities in motion, your accounts and their sales.

In Opportunities: your deals with follow-up, due date, close date, value, and probability in one row. In Accounts: every account with open opportunity count, Opportunity $ (pipeline), 2026 Sales YTD, 2025 Sales, and Last Activity — the whole money story per account.

Run the Reports — Service Labs & Construction Labs

The Labs are where the whole board comes into view — Pipeline, WIP, and Earned in one run, scoped to the business you live in:

S

Service Labs

The service side of the house: recurring work, T&M, and the service opportunities moving through your book. Run it to see your service Pipeline, WIP, and Earned together — and filter it down to your accounts and your territory before you read a single row.

C

Construction Labs

The project side: bids, awarded jobs, and the long-cycle money. Same three states, different rhythm — construction WIP moves slower and lands bigger, so this is the report that tells you whether next quarter is already sold.

The run is the same in both Labs: open the Lab → set your date range to YTD → filter to your accounts or territory → read the three states. In the live session we run both, on real data, and every attendee runs their own alongside.

Follow the Money — The Checklist

Run it this week, in Engage, on your own book:

0 of 6 — money's still standing still

Do / Don't

Do

  • Put every real deal in Engage the day it's real — value, service line, close date, stage.
  • Run Money in Motion — FSG Owner filtered to you — before every account conversation and pipeline review.
  • Double-click to fix fields right in the table. Click, correct, keep moving.
  • Read all three states together — Pipeline without Earned is a story with no ending.
  • Use the Labs to scope to your book first; the org-wide view is leadership's job.
  • Log the real won/lost reason — the win-rate machine is only as honest as you are.

Don't

  • Keep a deal in your head, your notebook, or your inbox. If it's not in Engage, it doesn't exist.
  • Leave a $0 value or a past-due close date on an open opportunity — that's an E or an LOB on the Closers vs Blanks board, with your name on it.
  • Confuse WIP with Earned. Sold isn't billed, and billed is what pays you.
  • Run a report unfiltered and drown in the org's rows. Scope first, read second.
  • Wait for your manager to pull your number. Closers know their YTD before they're asked.
