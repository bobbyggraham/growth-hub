Run enrichment and read the panel: full-match badge, New and Differs tags, per-field copy. Nothing changes until you copy it in — you stay in control of every field.

Third Base — Convert, and Read the Duplicate Screen

Convert to open the new account. Engage shows you possible matches before it saves — this screen is the whole episode in one moment. Exact match? Select it and attach. Nothing real? Save as New Account. Engage auto-assigns your Rep ID on convert — that's how it becomes your account.

Picked off.  Blowing past the duplicate screen is getting caught leaning off the bag — the error we watched in Episode 3's game film. A national account split in two, pipeline divided, site assignments orphaned. Read the screen. Every time.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Search Top Matches before creating anything — leads, contacts, accounts.
  • Enter every new relationship through Leads, even when you're in a hurry. Especially then.
  • Pull the title and details from the email signature while it's in front of you.
  • Read the four chips — Status, Sell, Owner, Class — before working any existing account.
  • Drop the deal's files in Files and notate what you need help with.

Don't

  • Add a bare contact straight to a generic master account and call it logged.
  • Save as New Account past an exact match — that's the turnover.
  • Hang a live deal on an inactive account without fixing activation first.
  • Name the account whatever the email subject said. Paper first, customer second.
  • Leave title, billing group, or hierarchy “for later.” Later is never.
