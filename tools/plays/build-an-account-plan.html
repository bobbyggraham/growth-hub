Build an Account Plan

You bring what only you know. Lumen builds the plan, loads Engage, and hands it back ready to work.
FACILITY SOLUTIONS GROUP
Growth Organization  ·  2026

Why This Play

You already carry the account in your head. Who picks up the phone, who blocked us last time, what they're about to spend money on. None of that is in a database — and all of it is what makes a plan worth reading.

So this form doesn't ask you to research anything. Lumen does the research. Public filings, footprint, buying signals, contact sourcing, competitor position — that's machine work. You fill in the ten minutes of truth only the person who works the account can give, hit send, and get back a full account plan built to Growth Hub standard with the Engage records already staged.

Ten minutes of your time buys a finished plan. Everything below is either an ID Lumen needs to find the account, or something Lumen cannot learn without you.

What Happens After You Hit Send

1

You submit

Fill the form, click Build my account plan. Your email client opens addressed to Bobby with you on copy. Paste, send. You keep the receipt.

2

Lumen goes to work

Account research, footprint and spend signals, contact sourcing with call flags, competitor read, and the play set — mapped to FSG business lines and products.

3

The plan gets built

Protect · Land · Expand, the play set mapped to FSG business lines and products, the opportunity set with every field that matters filled, a contact map with call discipline, and a 90-day sequence.

4

It lands in Engage

The finished plan is attached as a PDF in the account's Files tab in Engage — so it lives with the account, not in somebody's inbox. The account, contacts, and opportunities get staged against your name: follow-up dates set, close dates real, values entered. Open your dashboard and the work is already there.

How long does it take? It depends on demand and on the size of the opportunity — and this is going to be in high demand. Be patient. The tools are grinding for you.

How Requests Get Stacked

Lumen builds biggest opportunity first. The number that ranks your request is the realistic FSG opportunity across the account over the next 24 months — what FSG could actually win if the plan works, not what we bill today.

OpportunityWhere it lands
$5M or moreTop of the build stack. Full plan, contact sourcing, Engage load
Under $5MBottom of the build stack. Still gets built — behind every account that clears the bar
Be honest, not optimistic. A number you can defend in one sentence beats a big number you can't. An inflated request jumps the line once and costs you credibility on every one after.

The $5M line sets the order. Demand sets the speed. Nothing in the stack gets dropped.

What You Owe After

The plan is the start of the work, not the end of it. Once the records are in Engage they're yours — and they go stale in a week if nobody touches them.

D

Daily — Take Your Swings (5 min)

Open the Dashboard, work the top of your list. Follow-up date, close date, value. One touch and move on.

W

Weekly — Clear the Bases (Friday, 15 min)

No blank follow-up dates, no close dates in the past, before you leave for the weekend.

M

Monthly — Check Your Average (First Monday)

Walk the whole account. Replace every $1 placeholder Operations has since priced. Tell Lumen what changed and the plan gets rebuilt.

Common Misses

  • Account name that doesn't match Engage — Lumen builds a duplicate, and now there are two records and one honest forecast split across both.
  • No ERP ID — nothing joins to transacted revenue, so the plan can't show what FSG has already delivered for them.
  • An inflated opportunity number — it jumps the stack once. After that nobody trusts your requests, including the ones that were real.
  • "Nobody yet" left blank instead of written — a blank reads as an unanswered question and the request comes back to you. Write the truth and it moves.
  • Landmines left off — a DNC flag or a burned bridge that surfaces after the plan is built costs a rebuild and sometimes the account.
You know the account. Lumen knows the research. Engage keeps the score. Ten minutes here is the cheapest pipeline you'll build all month.
