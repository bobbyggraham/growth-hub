Why This Play Exists

The Growth Hub is not a document library. It's a drive — a sequence of moves that takes you from "I have an account" to "we won it, and the whole org can use the proof." Most people open the hub, see thirty cards, and close it again. That's not a content problem. That's a play-calling problem.

This is the play. Four stages, in order. Each one tells you the single question you're answering, the moves that answer it, and exactly which part of the hub to open. Run it once end to end and the hub stops being a shelf and starts being a system.

Start

"What is this thing?"

Get in, get on the list, and learn the story in under thirty minutes. One time only — you never do this stage twice.

Do

"Where do I point it?"

Pick one real account and build the plan. This is the stage people skip, and it's the one that decides whether the rest works.

Doing

"I'm in it — now what?"

Live in the deal. Say it so it sticks, log it so it counts, and answer "have you done this before" with something real.

Next

"How does this compound?"

Turn the win into ammunition for everyone else, then put your name on the board. The drive ends where the next one starts.

Tap any stage above, or use the rail at the top of the page to jump straight to it.

The Core Principle

The hub gives you back the two hours before the meeting and the twenty minutes after the win. What you do with those two hours and twenty minutes is the whole job.

This is the stage people skip because it feels like prep instead of selling. It is the stage that decides whether the meeting is a conversation or a pitch.
