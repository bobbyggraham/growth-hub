What · Three Services, One Program

Arc Flash Analysis

Know the risk. Protect your people.

  • Incident energy analysis per IEEE 1584-2018
  • Short-circuit & protective device coordination studies
  • NFPA 70E-compliant arc flash labels
  • Facility-wide PPE selection matrix
  • Updated as your system changes — 70E requires it

IR Thermography

See failures weeks before they happen.

  • Scanned energized, under normal load — no shutdown
  • Finds loose connections, overloaded circuits, failing breakers
  • Thermal images with severity classifications
  • Recommended corrective actions, prioritized
  • Fewer emergency calls, smarter maintenance planning

Switchgear Maintenance
