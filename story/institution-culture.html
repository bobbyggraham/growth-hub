Operating Principles

How we decide.

Principle 01
Customer first, always
Every decision answers one question: does this help the customer succeed? If not, we don't ship it.
Principle 02
Bias to action
Draft first, refine after. Speed of decision is a competitive advantage. We don't admire problems.
Principle 03
Own the outcome
No handoffs without ownership. If your name is on it, the result is yours — wins and losses both.
Principle 04
Tell the truth fast
Bad news travels first. We escalate before we explain. The cost of delay is always higher than the cost of candor.
How We Talk

The FSG voice.

Voice 01
Customer as hero
They succeed. We guide. Never "look how great we are." Always "here's how you win."
Voice 02
Outcomes, not features
Savings. Reliability. Speed. Lead every line with the result, follow with the proof.
Voice 03
Clear, not clever
Active voice. Short sentences. If a term needs explaining, explain it or cut it.
Voice 04
Confident, not arrogant
We're experts because we've done it. We're approachable because we know it's their building, not ours.
Rituals & Recognition

What we celebrate, repeats.

Ritual 01
Friday Wins
Five-line Slack post. New logo, expansion, save, referral, customer quote. Public, weekly, no exceptions.
Ritual 02
Loss Reviews
Every loss above $250K reviewed within 14 days. Not blame — pattern recognition. Lessons logged, shared, applied.
Ritual 03
Customer Quote of the Week
One actual customer line, posted to all-hands. Reminds everyone what we're here to do.
Ritual 04
Annual Summit Club
Top performers recognized in front of the company. Earned, named, remembered.
The Sales Team Standard

What "good" looks like.

Standards are quiet. They show up in how a forecast is built, how a customer email reads, how a teammate is treated. The team can describe them — because they live them.

Show up prepared
Every customer call has a written agenda and a researched stakeholder map.
Forecast like an owner
Commit only what you'll close. Sandbagging and over-promising are both disqualifying.
Make teammates better
Share the play. Open the deal review. The team's number is bigger than yours.
Honor the work
Field crews, project managers, ops partners — they're the reason we're invited back. Treat them that way.
Now / Next

Live one principle this week.

Pick one. Tell the truth fast. Or own the outcome. Or send the Friday Win. Find a way to be valuable — that's how we do good work.
