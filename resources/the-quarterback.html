Drive 1 · Daily rhythm

Your morning screen. Three checks before your first call.

  1. Tasks. What’s due today? Do it or move it — never let it rot on the list.
  2. Open opps. Which deal hasn’t moved? That’s today’s play call.
  3. Account alerts. A new contact, a new site, a problem on a job — every alert is a reason to pick up the phone.

Ten minutes, every morning. Quarterbacks don’t wing Sundays — they watch film all week.

Drive 2 · The relationships layer

Map the org. Ask about their job, not your scope.

Drive 2 · Stand and deliver

Break the huddle in five calls.

  1. Rapport — about them, 60 seconds. Asked about you? One sentence, then hand it back.
  2. Call the play — the win. “By the end of today, you’ll have…”
  3. Why it matters to them. A deadline, a cost, a complaint that goes away.
  4. Intros — by why they’re on the field. Theirs: “What’s the one thing you want out of today?”
  5. Hand them the ball. Then they talk 70% of the time.
