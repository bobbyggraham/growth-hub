The standard
Do what you said you were going to do — especially when you don't feel like it.

Summit is the achievement-award trip. The room you earn your way into. And on the free morning — the one with zero obligations, steps from the beach — this crew put on running shoes and showed up to train. Cones on the grass, a stopwatch, and people who hold each other to a higher standard. That's not a workout. That's who we are.

The morning nobody scheduled

Here's the thing about an off day: it tells the truth. When the calendar goes quiet, you find out what's actually built into a person. At Summit 2026 — the weekend FSG sets aside to recognize its highest achievers — the reward wasn't sleeping in. The reward was the people on that lawn, sweating together before the sun got hot, pushing each other through one more rep.

We weren't training for a race. We were training the habit of following through.

Look around that photo. Every person in it chose hard over comfortable, together over alone, standard over excuse. If you want to know why they were at Summit in the first place — that's why.

Put yourself in situations that require you to think, solve problems, and grow.

Surround yourself with people who hold you to a higher standard.

And do what you said you were going to do…

Especially when you don't feel like it.

That's how you build discipline. That's how you learn to trust yourself. That's how you become someone who can handle the challenges that come with the life you say you want.

You don't develop mental toughness by avoiding everything that requires it.

You develop it by facing those things and following through.

The coach: Todd Durkin & True Strength

Coach Todd Durkin · IMPACT-X

The man with the stopwatch was Todd Durkin — founder of Fitness Quest 10 in San Diego, two-time Trainer of the Year, and the coach pros like Drew Brees trusted with their careers. He runs IMPACT-X because he believes what we believe: peak performance isn't a body thing or a mind thing. It's both, trained together, on purpose.

But his résumé isn't the lesson. His story is. Todd was a quarterback chasing the pro dream when a back injury ended his playing career in his early twenties. The thing he'd built his whole identity around — gone. That valley could have finished him. Instead it became the foundation: he rebuilt himself, then spent the next three decades helping everyone from MVPs to first-timers do the same.

When life gets hard — that's when you discover True Strength. It's in the valley that you build the legs to climb the highest peaks.

True Strength — the valleys
Get Your Mind Right — the climb

He wrote the two halves of the same playbook. True Strength — 31 hard-earned lessons for overcoming life's most turbulent times — is about what the valley teaches you. Get Your Mind Right — 10 keys to unlock your potential, with a foreword by Drew Brees — is about how you climb out and keep climbing. Lost quarter? Deal that died at the finish line? Season of life that's just plain heavy? Same playbook. Face it. Follow through. Let the valley build your legs.
