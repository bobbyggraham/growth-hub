HOUSE RULES
An inning
One week. It closes Friday and the numbers go up.
A run
One closed win. Two wins in a week is a two-run inning.
A blank
A week with nothing on the board. It's not a callout — it's an inning that isn't over.
Average
Innings you scored in, divided by innings you played. Hit in half your weeks and you're batting .500.
The leader
Top average, ties broken by total runs. Gold rank, top of the order.
Cold
Blanked the last two innings. The average dims — it comes right back.
The season
A quarter. Then everybody starts even.
