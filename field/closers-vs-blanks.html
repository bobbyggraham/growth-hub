FSG CLOSERS VS THE BLANKS
 
OFFICIAL RULEBOOK — HOW THE GAME IS SCORED
An inning
One week. Friday's Pipeline Report is the post — whatever the export says is what went on the board.
An at-bat
One open opportunity in Engage. Every open opp is a trip to the plate.
A hit
An open opportunity with every field current — value, close date, stage, next step. A clean record is a base hit.
A blank
An open opportunity that isn't clean. Not a callout — it's an at-bat that isn't finished.
Average
Hits divided by at-bats. Keep a third of your book current and you're batting .333.
K
Strikeout — an open opportunity with no close date. No date, no plan, no swing.
LOB
Left on base — the close date has already gone by. The runner is still standing there.
E
Error — an open opportunity carrying $0 or a blank value. The dollars never made it onto the field.
On base
Dollars sitting in a real quarter — a close date in this quarter or ahead of it.
W · L · W%
Closed won, closed lost, and win rate across the window this week's export covers.
Corked
A win closed within 14 days of being created. It counts — it just never really took an at-bat.
Qualified
30 or more open opportunities. The batting-title minimum: you have to carry a real book to win one.
The season
Twelve innings. Then everybody starts even.

THE BATTING ORDER

FSG owners carrying a real book — 30 or more open opportunities. Batting title qualifiers.
