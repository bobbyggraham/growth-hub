Official Rulebook — How the Game Is Scored

How you make the Marquee: land a sign opportunity. The board tracks the sign opportunities loaded into the game — new sign opps opened since launch (11/10/2026) plus the active sign book added at each weekly post — by FSG Owner. Land one and your name goes up with a bulb for every sign opp you're carrying.

Win or loss, every light shows. Your bulbs: gold = closed won, dark = closed lost, hollow = still open. Nothing hides on a marquee.

The race: most sign opportunities closed won by Friday 1/15/2027 takes top billing; ties break on total sign dollars won. Wins and losses count from Nov 1, 2026 onward. Two leagues, one marquee: the Growth League (the Closers vs Blanks census roster) and The Sign Shop (FSG's sign-division sellers), each crowning its own Headliner.

Posting: innings post Fridays from the weekly signs export, diffed against the pre-launch book so only new lights count. The board is read-only; the DATA block in this file is the only source of truth.

The prize: the final Headliner in each league gets their name in lights — literally. Plus the handshake.
