Discovery — ask these on the first call

Each answer changes the price. Anything the buyer can't answer by proposal day becomes a written assumption in the proposal.

  • How many sites, and what are the street addresses and site contacts?A site list with codes only is not enough to price travel, permits or freight.
  • What does each site have today, and do you have photos?Existing sign type and size drive removal, disposal and mounting.
  • Non-lit, face-lit or reverse-lit — and is there power at the sign location today?Lighting adds LEDs, power supplies and possibly a new circuit.
  • What sets the letter height — the capital letters or the overall height?Changes letter size, weight and price on every set.
  • Where does the sign go on each building, and who owns the wall?Landlord, mall or airport-authority sign rules can limit size and placement.
  • Any new buildings in the program? Who is the general contractor and when is it ready?New builds need power, wall backing and pre-wiring from the GC.
  • How should we phase it — strict priority order or regional batches?Regional batches cut travel; priority order follows the business.
  • Is removal and disposal ours, and do you need recycling documentation?
  • Is a master agreement already in place, or does it sign with this scope?

Price it the way national buyers read it

Return the number in the buyer's own template when they send one. When they don't, use these five lines, each priced per site and totaled, with a per-site schedule behind it so survey findings have a baseline.

Cost lineWhat it carries
Site surveysSurvey crew and lift per site; the photo report and measurements.
FabricationLetter sets or sign cabinets, raceway or backer if used, LEDs and power supplies if lit, mounting kit.
Removal & installationCrew, lift or bucket truck, disposal and travel. Call out remote sites and new builds separately.
Packaging & freightEstimate per site; actual freight approved by the buyer.
PermitsAllowance per site for pulling and filing; the permit fee itself passes through.

Outside the base price, and named as such: permit fees beyond the allowance, change-order items found on survey, actual freight, and electrical work to bring power to the sign.

The warranty line. 10-year lifetime warranty on LEDs and a 5-year sign parts and labor warranty. Put both in every sign proposal.
