Seven questions that open the deal

1. "If a walk-in failed at 2 a.m., how would you find out, and when?"
2. "What's your setpoint and schedule standard, and how do you know a site is on it?"
3. "How many service calls last year ended with 'it was just a setting'? What does one cost?"
4. "What controls the HVAC today, who supports it, and what happens when it's offline?"
5. "What's rolling out across the portfolio in the next 18 months, and who specs the controls?"
6. "Where does energy show up on your P&L, and who owns that line?"
7. "If we surveyed five stores at no cost, which five would teach us the most?"

Never say · Always say

  • Never "guaranteed savings" or "ESCO." Always "measurable" or "verified" savings, proven with M&V.
  • Never a number inside the range. Always "budgetary; firm after the survey."
  • Never "we replace your system." Always "we can start with what's in the building."
