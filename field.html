How This Works

House Rules

The Season
A season is a quarter. Boards reset at the quarter, standings archive, and everybody starts even. Nobody carries a cold streak into January.
The Numbers
Every stat traces to Engage. If it isn't logged, it didn't happen — which is the point. The board is a byproduct of clean CRM hygiene, not extra work.
The Roster
The growth org, by name. Nobody is hidden and nobody is anonymous — a scoreboard with blanked-out names isn't a scoreboard.
Blanks
A zero is a zero. It's not a callout, it's an inning that isn't over. The whole design assumption is that a blank is the most fixable thing on the board.
Open Door
Open to the growth org, no passcode. The point of a scoreboard is that people look at it — a lock on the door defeats it.
New Games
A game needs one countable behavior, a live source, and a reason somebody would check it twice a week. Bring the behavior, we'll build the board.
Got a game? If there's a behavior worth counting and it lives in Engage, it can be a board. Tell Bobby what you'd put on the scoreboard and why people would check it.
